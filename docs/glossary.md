# Glossary

**Audit entry**: one change to a colour set or a product's Talasey settings, with who made it and how things were before and after. Restoring an entry puts things back as they were before it.

**Basket line**: one row in the basket: a SKU and quantity, plus what the customer chose (colour, dimensions, build).

**Build**: several SKUs added to the basket together for a build-your-own product (page type 4), such as a pergola.

**Catalogue editor**: an admin user with the `owner` or `catalogue` role, who can change colour sets, product settings and restore from the catalogue log.

**Colour set**: a reusable list of colours (name, hex value, photo) given to products of page type 1B or 2. Choosing a colour never creates a SKU or changes the price.

**Colour rule**: when a product's colour set applies, such as "show when Finish is Powder coated", and whether a colour must be chosen.

**Handle**: the part of a product's URL after `/products/`, such as `dusk-grey-porcelain-paving`. Range pages and guides refer to products by handle.

**Lead time**: how many working days a made-to-order item takes, from and to.

**Master sheet**: the product enrichment spreadsheet the catalogue is imported from, until a PIM replaces it.

**Page type**: one of 1A, 1B, 2, 3 or 4. It decides how a product is stored, priced, stocked and added to the basket.

**Pallet day**: the delivery day a customer holds at checkout for a pallet delivery.

**PIM**: product information management system. The master sheet plays this part for now.

**Range page**: the main page for one search keyword, such as `/porcelain-paving/`. It must show products or a launch list sign-up.

**SKU**: stock keeping unit: the code for one sellable item, such as SCRDL1800-600PC (design, size, finish).

**Stock mode**: stocked only, made to order, or stocked then made to order.

**Variant**: one SKU of a product, shown to shoppers as an option.
